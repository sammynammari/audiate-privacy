# Privacy Policy

*Audiate · Last updated October 23, 2026*

Audiate does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device
Your answers, scores and settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Network use
Audiate never connects to the internet. Every sound it plays is built into the app.

The credits link in Settings opens in your browser and is covered by that site's own policy.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
